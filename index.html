Women's Lives in Developing Countries

Introduction

In many countries, women and girls are treated very differently to men and boys. While men are seen as the breadwinners and decision makers in the household, women are seen as the caregivers and custodians. The following video is a reflection of the average day in the life of a girl in a developing country. While you watch, think about how it could be different to others.

Child Marriage

Child marriage is when young girls are married off to older men so that they can have children, preferably sons. The children have absolutely no choice in the matter, the parents organise everything with the other family.
