Women's Lives in Developing Countries

In many countries, women are not seen as their own people. Instead, they are viewed as the servants of men. Now, you may think something like this only happens in low income/developing countries, and for some part this is true. However, the amount of misogyny and gender inequality in highly developed/high income countries is frankly astonishing. Studies show that the UK is responsible for 4.41% of female unemployment in the world, closelly followed by the USA with 4.18% and Australia, with 4.05%.
