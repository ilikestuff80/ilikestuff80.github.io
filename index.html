Women's Lives in Developing Countries

Introduction

In many countries, women and girls are treated very differently to men and boys. While men are seen as the breadwinners and decision makers in the household, women are seen as the caregivers and custodians. The following video is episode one of our series: the patriarchy, a show that displays the difference in opportunities in these countries. Episode 1 is about the difference in everyday life between the two genders.
